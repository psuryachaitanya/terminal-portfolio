Presents a "pay-as-you-train" methodology for training large deep learning models on serverless cloud services, overcoming compute-time and data-size restrictions imposed by platforms like AWS Lambda and DynamoDB, with an analytical model comparing S3, Memcached, and DynamoDB as communication channels. Improves training performance and cost by 1.2x–5.5x.
