Proposes MetaFaaS, a function-as-a-service paradigm for scalable, cost-performance-optimal deployment of gradient-based meta-learning architectures (MAML, ANIL, ALFA), with an analytical model to predict cost and training time on cloud. Achieves a >5x training-time speedup on FaaS, and introduces eALFA, a compute-efficient variant achieving a >9x speedup over ALFA.
