An AI-driven cooking project using large language models and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) for accurate recipe generation and cooking assistance. Incorporated over 2 million recipes from the RecipeNLG dataset, plus Wikipedia and ingredient-price datasets, for improved recipe knowledge and more accurate cost calculations.
